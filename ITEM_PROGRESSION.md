# Item progression

## Tier map

| Tier | Name | Gate | Signature unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Foundational | Stone, flint, vanilla crops, Deepslate Crystal | Stone Spear, Crystal Dagger, first markers |
| 1 | Copper / Tin | Surface copper, Tin Ore, Copper Golem routes | Copper tools/armor, wire, lanterns, first machines |
| 2 | Silver | Silver veins and Forge vaults | Silver tools/armor, mana storage, backpacks |
| 3 | Platinum | Deep Platinum Ore | Platinum tools/armor, precision machines, alchemy |
| 4 | Mythril | Mythril below the deep line and Frost/Ember trials | Mythril gear, mobility, staffs, automation |
| 5 | Adamantite | Deep Adamantite seams and boss keys | Adamantite gear, breach keys, robust machines |
| 6 | Void / Lumen / Ember / Frost / Storm | Realm access and Forge stabilization | Elemental gear, Voidstone, portals, final restoration |

## Recommended route

1. Gather Deepslate Crystal, Copper Nugget, Tin Ore, and vanilla iron. Build Copper/Tin tools, Copper armor, Copper Wire, and a Forge ledger.
2. Find Silver Ore in deep stone. Smelt Silver Ingots, make precision tools, a Backpack, and the first Mana Crystal.
3. Use the Silver route to reach Platinum Ore. Platinum machines and armor make the first realm trials possible.
4. The Ember and Frost trials reveal Mythril seams. Mythril is a mobility tier, not a blanket replacement for every vanilla tool.
5. Defeat or outwit realm guardians to acquire Adamantite Ore and Boss Keys. Adamantite stabilizes physical structures near a breach.
6. Build the Lumen and Storm infrastructure, then assemble Voidstone, Portal Frame, and Portal Activator. The final tier is five elemental specializations with different costs, not five identical swords.

## Balance rules

- A higher tier raises durability or specialization, not every stat at once.
- Tier 6 actions use cooldowns, rare drops, or fuel.
- Food effects are short and low amplifier.
- Tech blocks communicate their role and never generate items for free.
- Spawn eggs cost a matching core or essence, while natural spawns remain rare.

## Content accounting

The catalog has exactly 200 entries. The 40 entries in Blocks & Building and Redstone & Tech, 8 world-generation ore/crystal entries, and three progression structures (Alchemy Table, Waypoint Stone, and Portal Frame) are native custom blocks; Bedrock automatically supplies their inventory form. The remaining 149 entries are portable custom items.
