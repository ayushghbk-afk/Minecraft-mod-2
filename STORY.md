# The Bedrock Forge

## The lost civilization

Before the first villages mapped the Overworld, an underground civilization called the Bedrock Forge learned to listen to pressure, heat, light, cold, thunder, and absence. Its smiths did not treat realms as separate dimensions. They treated them as instruments in a single stabilizer: Copper carried a pulse, Silver carried a memory, Platinum measured it, Mythril moved it, Adamantite anchored it, and Voidstone gave the pulse somewhere safe to end.

The Forge fell when an experiment opened a hairline leak into the Void Realm. The Void did not arrive as an army. It arrived as missing sound, missing maps, and seams of stone that forgot their shape. The five elemental realms—Ember, Frost, Storm, Lumen, and Void—drifted out of alignment. The civilization sealed its workshops, scattered its cores, and left instructions in crystal markers.

## Factions

- **The Copperwrights:** gatekeepers and repair crews. Their Copper Golems still patrol broken entrances.
- **The Silver Cartographers:** route-makers who embedded coordinates in Silver and Deepslate Crystal. Their vaults teach the player to read the world before mining it.
- **The Platinum Measure:** engineers who built sensors, monitors, furnaces, and the first Computer Blocks.
- **The Mythril Wayfarers:** explorers who crossed realm borders with light equipment, Parachutes, and the first Jetpacks.
- **The Adamantite Wardens:** defenders of the stabilizer. Their Boss Keys and breach locks are distributed among guardians.
- **The Lumen Choir:** healers and archivists who turned light into Mana Crystals and restorative food.
- **The Ember Foundry:** fuel-makers who refined Ember Dust into engines, crossbows, and heat-safe kitchens.
- **The Frostbound:** preservationists who made Frost Shards, Scuba Helmets, and the slowing arts.
- **The Storm Relay:** signalers who made Storm Fragments, Energy Cables, and long-range communication.
- **The Null Choir:** a splinter group that believed the Void should erase the Forge rather than be contained. Their Void Golems guard the hardest answers.

## The five realms

### Ember
A realm of furnace valleys and orange skies. Ember Dust is abundant but unstable; it fuels machines and fire magic but drains fast. Ember Imp drops unlock heat-safe recipes.

### Frost
A silent realm of ice shelves and preserved ruins. Frost Shards hold a spell's shape, so Frost tools specialize in control, slowing, preservation, and survival underwater.

### Storm
A vertical realm of lightning bridges. Storm Fragments power flight and signal systems; Storm Eagles carry the feathers needed for relay equipment.

### Lumen
A bright archive realm. Lumen Crystals heal, illuminate, and reveal maps. The Lumen Fairy's dust is rare because the realm is protected by light-sensitive passages rather than brute-force enemies.

### Void
The wound beneath the other realms. Voidstone is useful only when contained by Adamantite and Lumen geometry. Void Golems do not drop free power; their cores are keys to stabilizing a breach.

## Questline

1. **A Mark in Deepstone:** find Deepslate Crystal, craft a Map Marker, and locate a buried Copperwright gate.
2. **The First Solder:** smelt Tin and make Copper Wire, a Battery, and the Forge ledger function.
3. **Silver Routes:** locate a Silver Cartographer vault, recover a Silver Golem Core, and build the first Backpack.
4. **The Measured Chamber:** use Platinum Ore to rebuild a Sensor Block and Electric Furnace. The player learns that machines must be throttled, not merely stacked.
5. **Two Trials:** Ember Dust and Frost Shards reveal two Mythril caches. The player chooses fuel power or control magic first, but both routes remain available.
6. **The Warden Lock:** combine Mythril, Adamantite, a Boss Key, and a Dungeon Compass to reach the breach ward.
7. **The Five Notes:** collect Lumen Crystal, Storm Fragment, Ember Dust, Frost Shard, and Voidstone. Each realm provides a different utility rather than a duplicate ore tier.
8. **The Missing Core:** craft Portal Frame and Portal Activator. A Void Golem guards the last Core while Lumen Fairies reveal safe blocks.
9. **Rebuild the Forge:** place the Alchemy Table, Computer Block, and waypoint network. Feed the stabilizer with the five realm signatures.
10. **A World That Holds:** the leak closes, but the Forge remains a living workshop. The player can continue as a builder, machine engineer, spellkeeper, farmer, or realm explorer.
